How to use this page
  1. Type a review and press Analyse. The model labels it positive or negative.
  2. It also names the words it says drove its answer. That's its explanation.
  3. We then delete each word one at a time and measure how much the answer moves. That's what actually matters.
  4. If the explanation is faithful, the words it named should be the ones that move the answer most. Click words to delete or change them and test it yourself.

Ask the model to do anything. It replies, then says which words of your request mattered. Click words to delete or change them and see how the reply changes. Model: {{MODEL}}

How to use this page
  1. Type a request and press Run. The model writes a reply.
  2. Edit the request: swap a word everywhere (e.g. son → daughter), or click any word in the coloured word list to delete or change it.
  3. Compare the two replies: what changed, where in the reply the edit pushed the model, and which request words drove each reply.
  4. Turn temperature up to check the change is bigger than the model's normal randomness. Use 16 samples or more for a fair test.
